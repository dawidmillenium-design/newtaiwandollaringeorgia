TWN: The Taiwan Fund ticker and the currency-code mix-up

By Dawid Lisowski · Updated 29 September 2026

TWN has two very different contexts. It is the ticker symbol for The Taiwan Fund, Inc., a closed-end investment company. It is not the ISO 4217 currency code for Taiwan's New Taiwan dollar; that code is TWD. The Georgian lari is GEL.

The “TWN” displayed beside a coffee charge in my banking app does not mean I bought shares in the fund. A stock ticker and a payment currency field are different data. The issuer must verify why that label appeared on the transaction.

Where to find fund information

For the fund's strategy, reports, and investor information, use The Taiwan Fund's official site or the SEC filing page. Check a current market source for a price or net asset value before making an investment decision. This page does not display a live quote, chart, or volume feed.

Why the distinction matters here

The Batumi case file describes an unexplained “TWN” label on a reported card charge. The matching letters do not establish a connection to The Taiwan Fund. They also do not prove which system produced the label. The issuer's authorization and settlement records are needed for that answer.
