SGD to TWD: conversion and fee guide

By Dawid Lisowski · Updated 29 September 2026

The Singapore dollar uses SGD; Taiwan's New Taiwan dollar uses TWD. “TWN” is not the currency code for either. This page explains the conversion method and how to compare the rate you actually receive.

Calculate a conversion

Multiply your SGD amount by a current quoted TWD-per-SGD rate. For example, at an illustrative rate of 25 TWD per SGD, 100 SGD would equal 2,500 TWD before fees. That example is not a live rate or an offer.

For an actual transaction, check a current rate from your bank or payment provider and compare the final amount after its exchange markup, card fees, and any ATM or merchant charges. The amount shown at authorization can differ from the final posted amount.

Which code should appear?

A Singapore dollar amount should be labeled SGD, and a New Taiwan dollar amount TWD. The Batumi case file hub explains why an unexplained “TWN” label on a Georgian coffee charge deserves a direct question to the issuing bank. Do not infer an exchange rate from that label.

This page has no live feed or automatic calculator. Verify the current quote and final fees with your provider before converting money.

Follow the evidence

See the TWD outlook for broader currency context and the TWN ticker and TWD code distinction if a payment screen shows TWN. The Batumi case file documents one such display.

Evidence and limits: The calculation is illustrative. Check a current provider quote, fees, and final settlement for your transaction. Read the author's sourcing and corrections policy.
