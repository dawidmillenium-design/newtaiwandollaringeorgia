About the author and methodology

By Dawid Lisowski · Updated 29 September 2026

Who is writing

I am a writer and traveller documenting a payment display I observed after buying coffee in Batumi, Georgia. This is a personal case-file project. I do not represent a bank, payment network, fund manager, or merchant, and I do not claim professional financial or forensic credentials.

What the evidence shows

The Batumi case file describes what appeared in my banking apps and includes the images I chose to publish. The screenshots support the account of a displayed label; they do not establish why a processor or issuer displayed it. I distinguish that observation from interpretation and keep the cause open until the issuer provides a technical explanation.

The essay is a personal narrative. The ghost currency overview uses an informal term across several contexts. Neither should be read as a bank investigation.

How to check claims

For fund identity and reports, consult The Taiwan Fund's official site alongside the TWN ticker explanation. For a real exchange quote, use your own bank or provider, including its fees; the SGD to TWD example is illustrative. Company profiles and forecasts may become stale; verify dates and claims against current primary disclosures before relying on them.

Some older pages contain broad background descriptions and outbound references that have not been independently verified line by line. Treat those as context, not confirmed findings about the Batumi transaction.

Corrections and contact

To flag an error, open an issue in the public GitHub repository with the page URL, the exact claim, and a source. Please do not post account details, card numbers, passport data, or private transaction records in a public issue. Material corrections should be reflected in the page and its revision history.
