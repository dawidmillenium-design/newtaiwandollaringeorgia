The line that started it all

A coffee. One Georgian lari. A tap of the card, a beep, and the humid Black Sea air. Then, later that night, the banking app showed this:

Płatność kartą −1,00 TWN
georgian vending group, Tbilisi · Today
Dopasowana Mastercard debit ····••••

TWN is not a currency. It does not appear anywhere in ISO 4217. The real New Taiwan dollar is TWD; the Georgian lari is GEL. This hub collects the full case file, the essay, and every related article.

What to do when your statement grows a ghost

Keep the receipt. Check the posted amount and currency. Contact your card issuer promptly and ask which currency was authorized, which was settled, what exchange rate was used, and why the app displayed the unfamiliar code.

Honest caveat: the TWN display code remains unexplained. Only the card issuer's authorization and settlement records can confirm where it came from.
