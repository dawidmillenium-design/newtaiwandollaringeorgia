TWO CO-HOSTS ✦ ONE OCEAN ✦ LIVE

The 5-minute livestream script.

“Black Sea Devil From Tower” vs “Funky G from NYC” — The Million Dollar Border Deal · ~5:00 runtime

00:00 – 00:30

Cold Open — Black Sea Devil

Camera on Devil. Tower behind him. Beat low.

DEVIL: Yo. Two mics. One ocean. No studio. No label. Just a man, a phone, and the audacity, deadass. Tonight we walk the road — then we talk the deal. The million-dollar border deal. And we peep why evidence-based investigation ain’t built for the big green bag. Let’s get it.

00:30 – 01:00

Welcome — Funky G

Cut to Funky G, NYC, red cup on the table.

FUNKY G: BK in the buildin’, son. Devil, you been readin’ up on NYC a lot — trains, blocks, the whole plot. Tonight I’ma give you somethin’ real. The road. Then the deal. You ready?

DEVIL: I’m ready. Fill the gap.

01:00 – 01:30

The Setup — The Road

Split screen.

DEVIL: Here’s the frame for everybody watchin’: I read about NYC every day. But I wanna know — how does the road to York look? Not the postcard. The real thing.

FUNKY G: You want the real thing? Aight. Let me tell you what I peeped.

01:30 – 02:15

Round One — The Green Bag

Funky G leans in. Beat shifts.

FUNKY G: On the York road, I spotted somethin’ green. Huge, triple-packed — five kilos of tea. Cambodian, Vietnamese, Bangladesh — all three. That bag ain’t normal, son. That bag ain’t small. That’s a mad lot of leaf for one dude to haul.

DEVIL: Five kilos of green tea? That’s a lot of leaf. That ain’t a man’s breakfast — that’s belief. So what’s the word on the street? Why this tea bag got watchers all day?

02:15 – 03:00

Round Two — The Watchers

Funky G, slower, storyteller mode.

FUNKY G: Here’s the word on the block — and I’m just passin’ it down. Some cats try to breathe it and don’t come back around. Sleep for a few days. Wake up buggin’ out for a few days. Same game. So I peeped that bag — and I peeped the crowd. Military. Cops. The mob. Gangsters. P.I.s. All of ’em circlin’. So what’s a dude to do? I spark a blunt instead. Let the big green bag sit. I ain’t touchin’ it at all.

DEVIL: (laughs) That’s the road, huh? Big green bag, big watchers, and you stay clean. Aight — now the deal.

03:00 – 04:00

Round Three — The Million Dollar Border Deal

Cut to Devil. Then back to Funky G. Beat drops to half-time.

DEVIL: You been south, G. You been far. So keep it a buck — Mexican–USA border, many years ago. Millions of dollars. How’d that go?

FUNKY G: Devil, I’ll tell you. On the border, millions went through — that’s the fact. Not a contract. Not a lawyer. A handshake — and that’s that. The design was simple: trust, timing, and the road. One man on each side, and the rest is just code. Word is bond on the border — that’s the only law. You break it once, and the border don’t call you no more. So that’s the deal. Millions. Through. Clean. You know what I mean, man?

DEVIL: That’s the whole damn scene.

04:00 – 04:30

Round Four — White Magic On The Tower

Cut to Devil. Tower. Wind.

DEVIL: You ain’t asked me nothin’ yet — so let me drop one. What about white magic? Up here on the Tower. Is there white magic left? Is there any power?

FUNKY G: Good question, Devil. White magic on the Tower — what you got up there, son?

DEVIL: I’ll keep it a buck, no disguise. The only white magic here is snow. In the winter, for a few days — that’s the spell. White on the Tower, and then it gone. Apart from that? The magic got different colors here. Not white. Not clean. It’s a whole other tier. So that’s the Tower. That’s the road. That’s the deal. You know what I mean, man?

04:30 – 05:00

The Co-Sign + Call To Action

Both on screen. Beat builds.

FUNKY G: Then it’s border to the Tower — Mexico meets BK. One deal, two nations, two mics, one way. The million-dollar design — that’s the arc, that’s the frame. The road, the green bag, the handshake with no name.

DEVIL: No studio. No label. Just the trip and the talk. Black Sea Devil from the Tower — and the road we walked. That’s the intro. Now it’s time to have fun. Drop a 🔥 in the chat if you want the full song.

FUNKY G: And if you want the next battle — the funeral incense one, the happiness one — holla in the chat. You know what I mean, man? Let’s ride.

Beat drops. Stream rolls.
